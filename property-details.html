A Masterpiece of Modern Architecture

Perched atop the most coveted ridge in Bel Air, The Obsidian Pavilion is a tour de force of contemporary design. Conceived by award-winning architects, the residence seamlessly integrates the indoor and outdoor worlds through monolithic structural elements and expansive glass apertures.

The interior narrative is one of quiet luxury, where every surface—from the honed basalt floors to the custom rift-sawn oak cabinetry—has been selected for its tactile quality and enduring aesthetic. The open-plan living areas flow effortlessly toward the horizon, anchored by an infinity-edge pool that appears to spill directly into the Pacific Ocean.
